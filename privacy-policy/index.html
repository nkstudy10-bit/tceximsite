Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TC EXIM handles information provided through this website.

Information you share

When you submit a quote enquiry, we collect your name, email address, company, destination country, selected product, estimated quantity and the requirements you enter. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your enquiry.

How we use it

We use these details to review your sourcing requirements, respond to your enquiry and discuss a possible order. Enquiry details are stored privately in our website administration system and may also be sent to the business email used to handle enquiries.

Website operation

The enquiry form uses security tokens and a short-lived rate limit to help prevent abuse. Rate limiting uses a salted hash of the connection IP address, held temporarily for 15 minutes. The website hosting environment may also record technical access and error logs.

Access and retention

Enquiry information is available to authorised website administrators. We retain it as needed to handle the enquiry and related business records, subject to applicable obligations. Contact us if you want to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have supplied.

External links

If you follow a link to WhatsApp, social media or an externally hosted document, that service handles information according to its own privacy practices.

Contact

Use our contact form for privacy questions or requests. Clearly identify your earlier enquiry so we can locate the relevant record.
